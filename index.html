Datebook

Plan together.
Keep your story.

An app for two people. Collect the places you’ve both been meaning to try, vote on them without seeing each other’s picks, and keep every date you actually went on.

Collect

Share a place in from Maps, paste a link, or drop a pin. Ideas pile up between you instead of in one person’s head.

Vote

You each answer privately. Neither of you sees the other’s picks until you have both finished — so nobody is talked into anything.

Keep

Afterwards, one question per place and a photo or two. What you loved gets pinned to a map of the two of you.

In beta, on iPhone. Get in touch if you’d like to try it.
