Support

Questions, problems, or anything that looks wrong.

Email christinesavadel@gmail.com. It’s a small app and messages go to a real person.

The sign-in code hasn’t arrived

Check your spam folder first — codes come from an address you haven’t written to before, which is exactly what spam filters look for. If it still isn’t there after a few minutes, ask for another one, or sign in with Apple instead.

My partner can’t find me

Invitations are addressed to the email you signed in with, which you can see under Settings. If you signed in with Apple and chose to hide your address, theirs will need the invite code instead — also in Settings.

A photo isn’t showing

Photos are stored privately and load through links that refresh periodically. Closing and reopening the app is usually enough. If one is still missing after that, please write — it’s worth knowing about.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete my account. It removes your profile, photos, votes and notes permanently. Plans you made with someone stay with them; see the privacy policy for exactly what goes and what remains.
